A complete workflow

From Design To Analysis

Discover how you can take your electron optics simulations to the next level with our streamlined and versatile pipeline.

1

Geometry Builder

Create radially symmetric and 3D geometries with ease through our lightweight geometry module designed for ease-of-use and flexibility.

2

Mesh Generation

Transform geometries in structural meshes that preserve symmetries. For complex geometries, import your mesh using industry standard formats.

3

Excitation Setup

Easily assign voltages, currents, and material properties to mesh elements to configure the simulation for electrostatic and magnetostatic setups.

4

Field Computation

Solve electromagnetic fields efficiently with the Fast Multipole Method, enabling scalable simulations of detailed meshes.

5

Particle Tracing

Trace charged particles with high-precision time-stepping, with relativistic effects, space charge interactions and accurate axial field interpolations.

6

Beam Analysis

Analyze beam focal points and aberrations to assess optical performance. Use detailed insights to refine your designs.
