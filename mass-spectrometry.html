Application

Mass Spectrometry

Ion optics for time-of-flight, quadrupole, and sector instruments.

Design and analyze the ion optics behind modern mass spectrometers. Voltrace's unified electrostatic and magnetostatic solvers, together with relativistic particle tracing, help optimize ion transmission, mass resolution, and beam shaping across the full instrument.

Native magnetostatic support makes it straightforward to model hybrid electrostatic–magnetic elements—such as sector fields and magnetic analyzers—without resorting to external tools or ad hoc approximations.

  • Time-of-flight, quadrupole, and sector optics
  • Ion transmission and mass-resolution studies
  • Hybrid electrostatic–magnetic elements
  • Space-charge-aware particle tracing
